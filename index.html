Nido OS Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Application: Nido OS for Windows

Publisher: Comanche Labs (independent developer project)

Privacy contact: felipe.frias.pcs@gmail.com

1. Overview

Nido OS is a local-first desktop productivity application designed to help users manage tasks, reminders, callbacks, follow-ups, and conversations with an optional local AI assistant. This policy explains the information Nido OS may handle, how it is used, when information may leave your device, and the choices available to you.

This policy covers the Nido OS Windows application. It does not replace the privacy policies of Microsoft Windows, the Microsoft Store, or other software and services you separately choose to use.

2. Information handled by the application

Depending on the features you use, Nido OS may access and store:

  • Tasks, reminders, due dates, time zones, callback schedules, follow-up status, and notes.
  • Contact or lead information you enter or import, including names, telephone numbers, and related work notes.
  • Messages or instructions you type, editable speech-to-text transcripts, and conversation content that you choose to save.
  • Application preferences, such as appearance, assistant settings, and call-flow settings.
  • Information needed to show desktop notifications or support features that you expressly initiate, such as copying text or contact details to the clipboard.

Please only import or enter personal information you are authorized to handle. Do not use sensitive customer or regulated data in a testing build unless you have independently verified the relevant security and legal requirements.

3. Local storage and use

Nido OS is designed to keep its working records primarily on your Windows device using local application storage. The information is used to display and update your tasks, schedule reminders and callbacks, remember preferences, and carry out actions you request.

The current local-first design does not require a Comanche Labs cloud account to use its core task and reminder features. Nido OS does not automatically send imported lead lists, notes, or contact records to a developer-operated customer database as part of those core features. Local data can nevertheless be affected by your Windows account, device security, system backups, and other software installed on the device.

4. Microphone, audio, and transcription

If you activate a voice feature, Nido OS requests access to the microphone to capture speech and create an editable transcript using compatible local speech-recognition components. The intended current behavior is local audio processing without retaining raw microphone recordings as part of normal voice input. Text resulting from speech recognition may remain in the application when you choose to use or save it.

Microphone access is optional for users who prefer to type. You can deny or revoke microphone permission through Windows settings. Before release, the exact audio-handling behavior of the distributed build should be verified.

5. Optional AI features

Some features use deterministic, on-device commands without an AI model. If you enable a compatible local AI service such as Ollama, text you submit may be processed by a model running on your own computer. Installing models or related software may require contacting that software provider to download components, subject to its own policies.

The present Nido OS beta does not provide a general-purpose developer-hosted cloud AI account. If a later version introduces cloud AI, remote synchronization, external integrations, or additional data transfers, the relevant features and this policy must be updated before users are asked to rely on them.

6. Clipboard, notifications, exports, and external services

When you choose to copy a phone number, message, or other information, Nido OS may place that information on the Windows clipboard. If you paste or send it through another application, that application handles the information according to its own practices.

Nido OS may display reminder or callback details through Windows notifications while the application is running. Notification visibility and history may be controlled by Windows settings.

Data you intentionally export, share, or provide to separately connected services is subject to your actions and the receiving service's privacy practices. The current demonstration business integrations do not themselves authorize automatic transmission of customer information to a live CRM, dialer, email, or messaging provider.

7. Sharing, advertising, and analytics

Nido OS is not designed to sell users' personal information or display behaviorally targeted advertising. Its current core functions do not rely on a Comanche Labs-hosted user analytics account. We may receive information you voluntarily send when requesting support, such as your email address, screenshots, or diagnostic details, and use it to respond to your request.

Microsoft, Windows, installed dependencies, and any third-party software you choose to use may handle data independently under their own privacy terms. A website used to host this policy may also process basic technical information, such as IP addresses, for site delivery and security.

8. Retention and your choices

Local application records generally remain on your device until you change or delete them or remove the relevant local data. Where supported, Nido OS provides controls to view, edit, complete, or delete records. You can also control optional voice input, local AI connections, notifications, and certain app preferences.

Uninstalling a desktop application does not always erase its local databases, backups, exported files, or clipboard history. To request help locating or deleting Nido OS data on your device, contact the privacy address above. Do not send sensitive records or account credentials in a support request.

9. Security

Nido OS is designed to minimize unnecessary transmission of data and rely on the security controls of the Windows device where it runs. No application or storage method is completely secure. Protect your Windows account, install software updates, and avoid sharing the device or application data with unauthorized people.

The published release should be independently tested for its actual storage locations, permissions, optional integrations, and data-handling behavior. This policy does not promise encryption at rest, enterprise compliance certifications, or automatic deletion of every system backup.

10. Children's privacy

Nido OS is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal information directly to the publisher through a support interaction, contact us to request review and appropriate deletion.

11. Policy changes

We may update this policy when Nido OS changes, particularly if optional cloud features or third-party integrations are added. A revised policy will show its updated effective date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before materially different processing begins.
